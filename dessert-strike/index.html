Dessert Strike

Cute. Sweet. Deadly. Spend Sugar on unit cards, send your dessert army marching up the field each wave, and bring down the enemy's Wedding Cake before they bring down yours.

  • 14 dessert units across 3 tiers, from Gingerbread Men to the mighty King Donut.
  • Sugar Refineries, Sugar Chests, Nukes and Cookie Cannon turrets.
  • A 20-level campaign where every level has its own rules.
  • Vs mode against the computer on Easy, Medium or Hard, plus a Sandbox.

Coming soon to Google Play. Free, with no ads and no data collection.
