Privacy Policy

Dessert Strike · Last updated: November 2026

Dessert Strike is a single-player game for Android made by MoonSkeleton.

What we collect

Nothing. Dessert Strike does not collect, send or share any personal or device data.

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
  • The game does not ask for internet access or any other Android permission.

What stays on your device

The game saves your campaign progress and sound settings in a small file on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This file never leaves your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Children

Dessert Strike does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.
